# POV: Agentic AI Use Cases Need Portfolio Discipline

Agentic AI is creating a familiar enterprise pattern: many ideas, many pilots, and not enough sequencing discipline. The best use cases are not the ones with the most autonomy. They are the ones where autonomy, context, evaluation, and business accountability fit together.

## Leadership thesis

The right question is not "Can an agent do this?" The right question is "Should this workflow receive agentic capability now, and at what level of autonomy?"

## What enterprises miss

- They select agent ideas before mapping workflow risk.
- They give tools to agents before defining authority.
- They confuse reversible internal actions with material customer or financial actions.
- They evaluate task success but ignore approval compliance and rework.
- They scale pilots before business ownership is durable.

## Practical stance

Start with decision support and prepared actions. Earn higher autonomy through evidence: stable context, low rework, strong evaluation, clear approval patterns, and incident readiness.
