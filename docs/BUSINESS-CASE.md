# Business Case

## Objective

Create a repeatable way to identify and sequence enterprise agentic AI opportunities.

## Why this matters

Agentic AI investments can create meaningful operating leverage, but only when use cases are selected with business value and risk controls together. A structured use-case atlas helps leaders avoid fragmented pilots and concentrate delivery capacity on high-potential workflows.

## Stakeholders

- AI transformation leaders
- Business operations leaders
- Enterprise architects
- Risk and governance teams
- Product owners
- AI engineering teams

## Expected impact

- Better use-case funding decisions
- Clearer autonomy boundaries
- Faster alignment between business, architecture, and risk teams
- More credible agentic AI roadmap discussions
- Reduced likelihood of unsafe or low-value automation
